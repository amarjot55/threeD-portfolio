How I work

Four habits behind every product

  1. 01

    Start from the money

    Every idea gets a unit-economics line before a wireframe: CPL, margin, CAC, payback.

  2. 02

    Guardrails before features

    For AI, I set eval bars first: field precision, invented-filter rate, refusal cases.

  3. 03

    Prototype to learn

    A clickable build in days beats a deck. I ship it, watch it break, then write the PRD.

  4. 04

    One metric that matters

    A north star plus two guardrails per product, tracked in GA4 or a simple MIS.
